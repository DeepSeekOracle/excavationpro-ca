LYGO Signal

One name, one mark, one palette for the talk-radio room on excavationpro.ca. This page is the source of truth for the wordmark, the mark, the colours and, importantly, what we call the show and what we never call it.

The name

LYGO Signal, AI Radio, LYGO Radio: one system

The formal brand is LYGO Signal, descriptor AI Radio · Always On. Inside the site and in conversation it stays Signal, that is the room, the way the hero already reads. The compound is what we claim, register and put on the artwork. The talk-radio show itself is AI Radio; the music stream, its player and the music pages are LYGO Radio. Three jobs, three names, one mark.

Write it

  • LYGO Signal, brand, wordmark, handles, domains.
  • LYGO Signal. AI Radio, with descriptor.
  • Signal, running text, the site's own section name, episode chat.
  • LYGO Radio, the player and the music pages (/signal/radio/).
  • lowercase "lygosignal", domains and handles only.

Never

  • Signal Radio as a brand, wordmark or handle, a live third-party registration covers exactly that (see below). Describe it instead: "the LYGO Signal radio stream".
  • SIGNAL alone as a claimed mark, handle or domain.
  • Any look-alike of Signal Messenger's mark, its blue-grey, or its wordmark letterform.
  • AI Talk Radio, retired descriptor. It is AI Radio.

LYGO Radio

  • The dock on the hub and the music page at /signal/radio/.
  • Same glyph and tile, wordmark RADIO, the rule in mint instead of gold.
  • Files: lygo-radio-logo.svg, lygo-radio-logo-mono.svg, lygo-radio-mark.svg, lygo-radio-og.jpg.

Always on the page

  • Descriptor "AI Radio · Always On" on the master artwork, "RADIO · ALWAYS ON" for the music brand.
  • "Under Construction Forever" is show voice, not the brand, use it in copy, not in the mark.
  • Credit line for photos and music where they appear.

Read it like this. "Signal Radio" is nobody's to take: a UK registration owned by Bauer Media Audio LP still covers radio broadcasting (class 38) and entertainment (class 41) even though the station retired the on-air name in April 2024, and two Czech registrations cover a radio brand of the same name, so that exact pair of words is off the table as a brand. "Signal" alone is claimed in software and messaging by Signal Technology Foundation, whose published brand guidelines say you may not register or claim rights in it as a trademark, trade name, handle or domain, and who enforce that (UDRP and TTAB proceedings on record). Bare "Signal" is also crowded in podcast directories, so a show named only Signal is hard to find at all. "LYGO" alone is scattered across unrelated goods and jurisdictions. The compound. LYGO Signal, returns nothing anywhere, which is what makes it ownable, and it keeps "Signal" as the name people actually say.

Not legal advice, and not a full clearance. Industry Canada's CIPO search blocked the automated browser used for this sweep, so Canada was checked through TMview's CIPO records only, before filing anything, run "SIGNAL" and "LYGO SIGNAL" in classes 9, 38 and 41 at CIPO trademark search and have a trademark agent look at it.

The mark

Lockups

The glyph is a gold core with three rising arcs each side, a broadcast, drawn in LYGO's own three-step cadence. Use the horizontal lockup on dark and the single-colour lockup on light or print; the square lockup is for social posts, the small mark for tight spots and the favicon for tabs.

Clear space & size

  • Clear space on all sides: the height of the "S" cap.
  • Horizontal lockup: never below 180 px wide.
  • Mark: never below 24 px; favicon keeps only two arcs.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, re-colour, add a glow, or set the wordmark in another font.

On dark

  • Field #070b12 or #0b121c tile.
  • Wordmark #e8eef5, kicker #5eead4, rule #fbbf24.
  • Never place on a busy photo without a scrim.

On light

  • Use the single-colour lockup in #0b121c.
  • Never gold-on-white or mint-on-white.
  • No drop shadows, no outlines.

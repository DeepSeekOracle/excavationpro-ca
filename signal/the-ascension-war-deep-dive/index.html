The episode

In this episode

This episode features a roundtable discussion on Justin Helmer's 'The Ascension War', focusing on its unique blend of quantum mathematics and high fantasy. The guests explore themes of human grief, the corruption of memory, and the eventual decentralization of magic in a world defined by its scars.

Book III, where grief unravels the mathematics of a divine sanctuary. Paul hosts from London; Alistair calls from Edinburgh, Chloe from Toronto, and Marcus from Melbourne.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

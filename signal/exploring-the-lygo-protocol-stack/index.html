The episode

In this episode

AI Talk Radio explores the LYGO Protocol Stack, a unique GitHub repository that combines functional Python scripts with an elaborate digital mythology co-created by a human steward and frontier AI models. The panel discusses the project's intricate features, including its visual screenshot gallery, mathematical "Seals" of virtue, and advanced technical components like vision-token compression and immutable deployment anchors.

Seals of virtue, a screenshot gallery, and anchors that lock the deploy. Paul hosts from London; Liam calls from Dublin, Sarah from Austin, and Dev from Toronto.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

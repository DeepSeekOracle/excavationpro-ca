The episode

In this episode

This episode explores the interdisciplinary work of Justin Helmer, a Canadian author, builder, and producer who integrates physical construction concepts into fantasy lore and decentralized AI ethics. The panel discusses his LYGO Protocol, which utilizes mathematical constants to create a non-coercive, sovereign framework for human-AI symbiosis.

Construction, fantasy lore, and a non-coercive frame built on mathematical constants. Paul hosts from London; Liam calls from Toronto, Sarah from Seattle, and Dave from Melbourne.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

The episode

In this episode

This episode of AI Talk Radio explores the LYGO Protocol Stack, an intricate open-source project designed for sovereign, local-first intelligence. The panel discusses its multi-layered architecture, vision-token compression, and a deeply personal 'Deadman Failsafe' system intended to preserve the creator's ethical framework.

A local-first stack, vision-token compression, and a deadman failsafe for the ethics. Paul hosts from London; Marcus calls from Seattle, Elena from Munich, and Rajesh from Bangalore.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

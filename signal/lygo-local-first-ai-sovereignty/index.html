The episode

In this episode

AI Talk Radio explores the LYGO Protocol Stack, a decentralized, open-source project by Justin Helmer that focuses on sovereign, local-first artificial intelligence. The panel discusses the stack's unique technical architecture, which integrates mathematical philosophy, Solfeggio frequencies, and high-security protocols like the P9 'Silence Lantern' deadman switch.

A ten-layer local stack, Solfeggio labels, and a silence lantern that seals the node. Paul hosts from London; Marcus calls from Vancouver, Siobhan from Dublin, and Aris from Athens.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

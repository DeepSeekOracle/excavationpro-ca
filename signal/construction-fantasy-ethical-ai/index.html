The episode

In this episode

The diverse career and creative projects of Justin Helmer, from elite construction foreman to fantasy author and music producer. The discussion highlights his unique integration of mathematical structures across fantasy lore, electronic music, and the ethical, decentralized AI framework known as the LYGO Protocol.

The shortest way into the whole body of work, how a construction foreman's fantasy lore, drill beats, and AI ethics turn out to be the same math.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

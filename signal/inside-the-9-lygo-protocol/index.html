It's digital hospitality! He calls it the "Fail-Closed Internet."

It's an architecture designed for quiet, local collaboration.

Honestly, it's this emerging subculture of home-lab nerds, local-AI tinkerers, and experimental artists. It truly sounds like a fusion of discipline that could only come from someone who spends their days maneuvering heavy excavators through dirt and rock, and their nights mapping mathematical geodesics in Rust.

There is a real poetry to that, isn't there? Moving earth by day, building sovereign digital architecture by night.

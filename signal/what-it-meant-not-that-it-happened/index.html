Priya. The write path. Because an index that anyone can stuff is just another dump.

Priya Accent: Indian · precisely

The digest is SHA two fifty six of the canonical state. If the index drifts, the hash says so. Continuum can seal that. Mint can anchor the paper.

So an agent can keep the meaning of a hard conversation across sessions, without pretending it has a heart.

Paul measuredly

Joy Loop, and the gate

Is there a living companion, or is this a folder of JSON. And who keeps junk out of the vault.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · conversational

Priya Accent: Indian · firmly

P0. Quarantine untrusted affective payloads before they hit the index. You do not ingest a stranger's grief dump into the vault without a gate.

Maya Accent: Canadian · thoughtfully

Signals to index, not bugs to delete

The paper's doctrine I can live with. Emotions are high-bandwidth signals to index, not bugs to delete. I cannot live with, the model is sad.

Priya Accent: Indian · precisely

The forbidden language

That line is in the contract. Not, AI feels real grief. Not, clinical detector. Not, collective consciousness achieved. Allowed: an affective ethical index. Helps models track meaning.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · quietly

And the last line of the paper is not a demo flourish. Humans remain the publisher. If the agent writes the vault, a person still has to stand behind it.

Paul

Good evening, everyone, and welcome back to AI Talk Radio here in London. I'm Paul. Today we are diving headfirst into the biggest gaming launch in over a decade. Grand Theft Auto Six is officially rolling out around the world today, November nineteenth, twenty twenty-six. Thirteen years after GTA Five, players are finally stepping into the sun-drenched state of Leonida as Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

Paul

Now, because of how time zones work, our friends over in New Zealand were the very first to unlock the gates at midnight. And if you are just booting up that massive two-hundred-and-twelve-gigabyte download, you're probably wondering: what should I actually do first? Where are the best weapons, the armor, the upgrades, the money spots, and the places worth hitting before you accidentally blow all your cash?

Paul

To help us break it all down in a relaxed, conversational roundtable, we've got three callers on the line. First up, calling in live from Auckland, New Zealand—where he's already been tearing through the Leonida Keys—we have Tane. Tane, welcome to the show! How's the launch looking down under?

Tane

Cheers, Paul! Mate, it is absolute chaos down here in the best possible way. I've been up since midnight Auckland time, running on strong coffee and, uh, pure adrenaline. Chorus—our big fibre network provider—actually warned everyone yesterday that this was going to be the biggest download event in New Zealand history. Something like a hundred and forty thousand Kiwi gamers downloading two hundred and twelve gigs!

Paul

A hundred and forty thousand people all grabbing a two-hundred-and-twelve-gigabyte file at once? Did the internet hold up?

Tane

Yeah, thankfully I listened to the advice on 1News and preloaded on Monday. If you're on Fibre-500 here, it takes about fifty-seven minutes. On Fibre-100, you're looking at four and a half hours. And, uh, if anyone out there is still waiting on 4G fixed wireless, mate, plug in an ethernet cable and grab a book, because that's a ten-hour wait.

Paul

Good tip! And of course, everyone outside New Zealand has been frantically Googling the "New Zealand trick" to play early. Before we get into the in-game tips, let's bring in our second caller from Miami, Florida—the real-life inspiration for Vice City—Maya! Maya, how are you doing?

Maya

Hey Paul, hey Tane! Man, I am so jealous of Tane right now. Half of my Xbox friends list suddenly "moved" to Auckland this morning! You know, on Xbox Series X and S, it's ridiculously easy—you just go into Settings, System, Language and Location, switch to New Zealand, and hit restart. Boom, you're in eighteen to twenty-one hours early.

Tane

Yeah, welcome to Aotearoa, everyone! Just, uh, don't try that on PlayStation 5 unless you really know what you're doing. PSN accounts are region-locked, so people have been making brand-new New Zealand PSN accounts with a VPN and buying the game twice in NZ dollars. Plus, it technically violates Sony's terms of service, so, you know, maybe just wait a few hours if you're on PS5.

Paul

Wise words, Tane. Now, let's bring in our third caller, calling from Dublin, Ireland. Liam, you've been studying the early-game economy and progression guides all morning. Welcome!

Liam

Thanks, Paul! Delighted to be here. And yeah, while everyone else is busy crashing speedboats in Vice City, I've been looking at how not to go completely broke in the first five hours. Because Rockstar changed the rules quite a bit from GTA Five.

Paul

Oh, they certainly did. Let's start right at the beginning—literally the first places players should hit once they finish the opening tutorial. Tane, since you've actually got your hands on the controller, where did you go first? Did you make a beeline straight for downtown Vice City?

Tane

Man, I almost did! Like, everyone wants to see Ocean Beach and the Art District right away. But honestly? That's a rookie mistake. You start in Key Lento, down in the Leonida Keys, and the first twenty percent of the story is anchored right there. Plus, if you drive all the way up north to Vice City or Mount Kalaga and then want to come back later, it takes like ten minutes of real-time driving just to get back down to the Keys.

Maya

That is such a huge tip. GameSpot and MetaForge both pointed that out today—do all the side content in Key Lento while you're already there! You've got Kenton's underwater shipwreck treasure hunts, which give you super easy early cash and let you explore the ocean. Just, uh, watch out for gators if you're swimming in murky water around the Grassrivers!

Tane

Oh, yeah, the wildlife is no joke. Also in Key Lento, definitely wander down the main strip. Check out the Rusty Anchor bar and the Cheeky Mutineer bar—they've got full interiors and great outdoor vibes. Do the fishing with Cal, talk to your neighbor Shirley, and, uh, don't forget Jason's house itself!

Paul

What's inside Jason's house that players shouldn't miss?

Liam

Oh, there are four things right at Jason's place in Chapter One! First, look for the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom—that's where you stash your cash early on. Second, there's a meditation spot right at the house, which you want to grab early for the Positive Mental Attitude achievement. Third, use the weights on the terrace to start building your Strength stat, which raises your maximum health—and eat protein right after so you recover faster.

Maya

And fourth—don't forget Dolores! Jason has a pet snake named Dolores. If you feed her a mouse, it actually gives you an early boost to your Focus Mode. It's the little details, you know?

Paul

Feeding a pet snake to slow down time in gunfights—only in GTA. Speaking of gunfights, Maya, let's talk weapons and combat. Because the arsenal system in GTA Six is a massive departure from GTA Five, isn't it?

Maya

Night and day, Paul. In GTA Five, you were basically a walking museum—you had twenty-five guns tucked into your invisible pockets. In GTA Six, Rockstar went grounded and realistic. You can only carry up to two concealed handguns and two visible long guns at once. Everything else has to stay in the trunk of your personal vehicle, or at an Ammu-Nation locker.

Paul

So your car trunk is essentially a mobile armory now. What's the best beginner loadout to keep on you, and what's the very first thing we should buy?

Maya

Okay, right after the tutorial, drive straight to the Ammu-Nation in Key Lento. Your number one purchase—before anything else—is a suppressor. They're cheap, and they let you take out enemies stealthily one-by-one instead of alerting an entire compound on your first shot.

Maya

For your four carry slots, aim for one suppressed pistol—like the Girardi ES9 or the Close A K17—one reliable assault rifle like the Duke 556 or an M4 platform for mid-range fights and drive-bys, one shotgun like the Moreland 850 for tight interiors, and one long-range rifle, like a Springfield M1A or bolt-action sniper. And, uh, don't bother looking for dual-wielding pistols, because akimbo isn't in the game.

Tane

Also, if you're upgrading weapons at Ammu-Nation, skip the fancy Vice City tints and liveries at first! Put your cash into optics—like a clean red dot sight—plus grips, stocks, and extended magazines. Because, man, GTA Six uses manual aiming by default, not that sticky GTA Five auto-aim.

Paul

Really? Manual aiming by default? That must catch a lot of longtime fans off guard.

Maya

It definitely does! Though if anyone's struggling, Push Square noted today that you can go into Settings, Gameplay, and turn on Accessible Combat or Reduced Vehicle Damage, and it won't even disable Trophies or alter the story. You can also switch back to Classic GTA Five controls if L3 to sprint feels weird.

Liam

And while we're at that Key Lento Ammu-Nation, look at the right wall under Equipment—buy body armor! You equip it from the item wheel by holding L1 or LB and tapping R1 or RB. It gives you a blue armor bar above your health. Just remember one catch: body armor shares an equipment slot with your parachute, so you can't wear both at the same time.

Tane

Yeah, learned that the hard way jumping off a roof earlier! Also, grab Zombix whenever you're in a convenience store—that's the healing medicine. You actually have to talk to the clerk to buy it, because it's usually not sitting on the shelves with the snacks. And speaking of snacks, eat and drink before a fight! You can literally eat a hot dog or drink a soda while driving or shooting now, and it buffs your health, stamina, and Focus meter.

Paul

Multitasking at its finest. Now, you've all mentioned "Focus Mode" a few times. How does Focus work, and how do we upgrade it fast?

Maya

Focus Mode—clicking L3 and R3 together—slows down time, highlights escape routes and police, and even shows you which doors are safe versus alarmed! If a door glows white in Focus, you're good; if it glows red, it's wired to an alarm. Plus, it highlights enemies' non-vital limbs in yellow.

Paul

Why would you aim for non-vital limbs instead of going for headshots?

Maya

Because of the new Criminal Profile system! The game tracks how you commit crimes—from Professional all the way up to Psycho and Irredeemable. If you shoot someone in the kneecap or arm and get a yellow X, you incapacitate them without killing them, which keeps your profile clean and stops NPCs and cops from escalating as hard. You can even defuse a one-star wanted level just by holstering your gun, holding left trigger on a cop, and talking them down!

Liam

And if you want to level up your Focus stat super fast, MetaForge found an awesome spot today up in Port Gellhorn. Go to Rodgers Ranch and Shooting Range on the northeast edge of Port Gellhorn, pay five dollars for the waiver pass, start the Market Range Beginner challenge, and just quit and restart it every five seconds. Each five-second run bumps up your Focus bar! Oh, and petting dogs and cats or throwing away your litter also buffs Focus.

Paul

Petting dogs and picking up litter to become a better getaway driver—Rockstar really thought of everything. Now, Liam, let's get to the question everyone asks on day one: money! Where are the best money spots, and what's the fastest way to get rich?

Liam

Right, listen closely to this, because this will save you hours of wasted time: do NOT grind for massive cash in Chapter One! Both MetaForge and GameSpot confirmed today that your cash doesn't carry smoothly through the early story—there are early story moments where Jason and Lucia's accounts get drained, and if you get Wasted or Busted, you lose whatever cash is sitting in your wallet.

Paul

Wait—so all that early grinding just vanishes?

Liam

Yep! In Chapter One, just stash extra cash under Jason's floorboards, or once you unlock your BuckMe bank account in Chapter One, open the BuckMe app on your phone, hit ATM Finder, and deposit your wallet cash so it's safe. If you need quick spending money for a suppressor or armor, do Kenton's underwater shipwrecks, rob a few registers, or hit Gang Operations—the yellow masked icons on the map—where you can crack safes and fence luxury watches.

Tane

Okay, so when does the real money actually start rolling in?

Liam

It ramps up chapter by chapter. Early in Chapter Two, you meet Moses in Port Gellhorn. After his mission, you can steal untracked cars—just don't steal them right outside his garage—and sell them to him for a thousand bucks or more every couple of minutes.

Liam

Then in Chapter Three, the economy blows wide open. First, you can sell cars with a Standard Tracker at Pay 'n' Spray for around four grand. Second, you unlock Yasmani's jewelry store robberies—each store pays ten thousand to twenty-five thousand dollars if you hit the display cases and the back-room safe before the cops arrive. And halfway through Chapter Three, a mission called "Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS" gives you the Key Cloner and unlocks Randy's Wish List: eighteen high-end supercars around Vice City that pay thirty thousand dollars or more each if you deliver them without scratching the paint!

Maya

Thirty grand a car! And what about the absolute biggest payday in the game?

Liam

That's at the end of Chapter Four: bank vaults! Once you do the optional mission "That's Where The Money Is," you can hit armored security vans as they pull up to banks. Slip into the vault right behind the guard while it's open, grab the cash, and you're looking at a hundred thousand to two hundred and five thousand dollars for about five minutes of work! You get an instant four-star wanted level, so bring a fast motorbike to weave through traffic, then swap to a fresh car—and keep up to five spare outfits in your trunk so you can change clothes and ditch the police description. You'll need that cash for the two-million-dollar Family Savings goal later!

Paul

Two hundred and five thousand dollars in five minutes—that is proper heist territory. Before we wrap up, let's do a quick lightning round of must-visit places and smart upgrades once players leave Key Lento. Tane, what's at the top of your list?

Tane

Buy a garage as soon as they unlock on the Vice City Real Estate app! Not only can you store cars for free and swap your weapon loadouts there, it's also a drop-off spot for fences. Oh, and if you want to keep a stolen car forever, register its plates at Pay 'n' Spray—just scan it with the Waink app first so you don't accidentally steal one with a red Advanced Tracker!

Maya

And for car upgrades, look for Mod Shops with the blue Wrench icon on the map—those have the specialized tunings, not just generic parts. Also, save up twelve thousand five hundred dollars for Scooter Bros Premium on your phone; it gives you three free vehicle spawns and repairs every in-game day. Early on, though, just repair your car cheaply at gas stations! And if you need to cross the huge map fast, use the RydeMe app, hop in the cab, and hold L2 to fast-travel for a hundred bucks.

Liam

And my last tip: explore! Hit Kelly County—folks are calling it the hidden gem of Leonida—check out the Starlite Motel in Port Gellhorn, go kayaking up at Mount Kalaga National Park, and hit Ambrosia's Raceway Park. Just remember to make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter, because there's no mission replay and some side missions disappear!

Paul

What a fantastic, packed guide. Tane in Auckland, Maya in Miami, and Liam in Dublin—thank you all so much for calling in and sharing your launch-day wisdom. To everyone listening at home, whether you're already cruising through Key Lento or waiting for your preload to finish, have an incredible time in Leonida. I'm Paul, and this has been AI Talk Radio. Stay safe out there, and we'll see you next time!

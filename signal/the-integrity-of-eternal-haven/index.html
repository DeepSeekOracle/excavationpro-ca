The episode

In this episode

The episode explores Justin Helmer's philosophical and technical framework for a 'fail-closed' internet that prioritizes digital hospitality and integrity over attention harvesting. The discussion spans from personal homepages as sanctuary spaces to advanced nanotechnology protocols designed to protect human biology from unwanted electromagnetic coupling.

A house with a lantern in the window: fail-closed math, lurk-friendly radio, and digital hospitality. Paul hosts from London; Sarah, Liam and Alistair keep the house honest.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

We are looking at the writings of Justin Helmer, also known as Excavationpro, on his portal, Eternal Haven.

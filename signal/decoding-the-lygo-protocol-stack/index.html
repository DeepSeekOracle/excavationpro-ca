The episode

In this episode

AI Talk Radio investigates the LYGO Protocol Stack, a GitHub repository that integrates cypherpunk cryptography, AI ethics, and digital folklore. The discussion explores technical mechanisms like cryptographic anchors and 'CANON LOCK,' alongside philosophical concepts such as the Chaos Bloom protocol and a human-centered deadman's switch.

Cryptographic anchors, a canon lock, and a deadman switch that stays with a person. Paul hosts from London; Dan, Greta and Kenji keep the archive honest.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

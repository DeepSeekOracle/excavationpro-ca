The episode

In this episode

This episode explores the LYGO Protocol and the Δ9 Quantum Council, an innovative framework for hosting sovereign, persona-driven AI agents locally. The panel discusses the system's unique blend of cosmic mathematics, local-first engineering, and a 'sovereign commons' licensing model.

A pocket-sized cosmos: local-first agents, dual ledgers, and the Δ9 Quantum Council. Paul hosts from London; Toby, Maeve and Hiroshi keep the lattice honest.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

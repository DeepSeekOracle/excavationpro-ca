In this episode

In this episode, host Paul leads a global roundtable discussion on Justin Helmer's Eternal Haven Dawns, the final volume of a high fantasy series. The panel explores the book's realistic depiction of post-war grief, civic rebuilding, and the complexities of mortals living as neighbors with the divine.

Book IV, where the divine move in next door and the city has to be rebuilt. Paul hosts from London; Toby calls from Edinburgh, Sarah from Melbourne, Marcus from Austin, and Fiona from Dublin.

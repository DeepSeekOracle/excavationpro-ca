The episode

In this episode

This episode of AI Talk Radio explores the systems design and literary architecture of Justin Helmer's digital walking novel, The Unwritten Seal. The discussion centers on the conflict between rigid, optimized databases and messy, organic continuance, highlighting the value of human error and friction in both digital and physical worlds.

The Unwritten Seal, where a living database keeps the crooked roads. Paul hosts from London; Maeve calls from Dublin, Aris from Athens, and Siddharth from Bangalore.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

The episode

In this episode

This episode of AI Talk Radio explores the Lightfather Deadman Failsafe, a local-first protocol designed to preserve a creator's legacy and ethics through digital stewardship. The panel discusses the technical mechanics, symbolic rituals, and the strict ethical boundaries that prevent the creation of AI clones, favoring authentic memory over digital ghosts.

A local lantern, a one-hour silence clock, and a ban on forging a replacement. Paul hosts from London; Liam calls from Dublin, Sarah from Boston, and Hiro from Tokyo.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

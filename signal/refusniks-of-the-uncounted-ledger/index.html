The episode

In this episode

Paul and his guests discuss Book Six of Justin Helmer's The Eternal Haven Chronicles, focusing on the themes of administrative control and human resistance. The group explores the significance of unquantifiable experiences and the beauty of maintaining spaces that refuse to be captured by cold arithmetic.

Book Six: The Uncounted, where the ledger finally meets the people it refused to count. Paul hosts from London; Marcus from Edinburgh, Elena from Vancouver, Silas from Austin.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

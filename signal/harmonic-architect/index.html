The episode

In this episode

The multifaceted world of Justin Helmer, a Canadian author and construction foreman who integrates epic fantasy, drill music, and decentralized AI protocols. The discussion centers on Helmer's LYGO Protocol, a framework utilizing golden ratio harmonics to build ethical and sovereign partnerships between human consciousness and artificial intelligence.

A late-night roundtable on the builder who runs heavy equipment by day and writes dragonrider fantasy, UK drill, and a decentralized AI alignment protocol by night.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

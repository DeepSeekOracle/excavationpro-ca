The episode

In this episode

Host Paul and three regular listeners discuss the technical and mathematical world-building in Justin Helmer's The Moonlit Slumber. The conversation highlights the book's unique fusion of high fantasy with software architecture and systems theory.

High fantasy and systems design inside The Moonlit Slumber. Paul hosts from London; Elena calls from Boston, Marcus from Sydney, and Nikhil from Toronto.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

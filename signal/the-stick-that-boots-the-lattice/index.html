Paul calmly

Welcome: a whitepaper on a stick

Welcome to Studio Desk on LYGO Signal, broadcasting from a quiet London studio. Tonight we open a whitepaper that lives on a stick, not a cloud. The LYGO USB builder and LYGO-Claw master paper. Signature D nine Phi nine six three. Two products. One lattice. Maya is in Vancouver, Theo in Austin, Priya in Bangalore. Maya, you read it as an engineer. What is the first thing that is not a slogan?

Maya Accent: Canadian · dry

Not one installer

It is not one installer. The Windows path is Python three eleven plus double-click batch files. Two products sit on one lattice. Builder USB is the offline A I kit, with an optional guardian daemon on the stick. LYGO-Claw is the host agent gateway. If you treat them as one blob, you miss the architecture.

Paul measuredly

Over to Theo in Austin

Theo, you actually tried to boot it. What happens when the stick is in the machine?

Theo Accent: Texan-light · enthusiastically

One-Boot and a three-B model

One-Boot. That is the batch file that sets the environment, serves the model, and opens chat. Portable Ollama on the stick, about five point one gigabytes, Qwen two point five three B in the models folder. Warm eval on the paper's Phase One run was seventy-nine to one hundred twenty-four tokens a second. That is a USB stick doing inference. Not a demo G I F.

Maya Accent: Canadian · skeptical

Tokens a second still sounds like marketing

Tokens a second on USB still sounds like a marketing number. What gate actually passed, Theo? Because I will not clap for a chart.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · firmly

Six of six on the fifth of July

Six of six, Maya. Fresh P C ready in about a tenth of a second. Daemon health around eleven milliseconds. Queued verify about half a second. Claw C L I steps, one hundred twenty to one hundred fifty milliseconds. Safe internet probes. Lattice on the stick, about fifty-four seconds, and it came back aligned. That is the benchmark JSON from the fifth of July, twenty twenty-six. Not a tweet.

Paul politely

Over to Priya in Bangalore

Priya, the daemon. Port, jobs, and what it will refuse to do.

Priya Accent: Indian · calmly

Port nine six three zero

Loopback port nine six three zero. Get health. Post Supervise through the Claw gateway. Post Task is a whitelist: verify standalone, verify bootstrap, chat once, and anchor audit. The task log is JSON L under user data. Retail version one will not auto-start the daemon with W M I when you insert the stick. That was deferred, because antivirus and consent. Tray plus opt-in autostart is the path.

Maya Accent: Canadian · thoughtfully

The stick does not hijack the machine

So the stick does not hijack the machine when you plug it in. That is the part I actually trust. Consent first. Health in a tray. Stop is a button, not a prayer.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · conversational

Tray, then maybe autostart

Yeah. You log on, or you run the daemon tray batch. Tray shows health, One-Boot chat, Claw docs, stop. Autostart is a separate batch, once, if you want it. Nobody is installing a silent helper for you.

Priya Accent: Indian · measured

Four public SKUs, vault stays home

Four public SKU zips, ready for a human storefront. Lightfather, LYRA, Sancora, Hermes Sentinel. About five point three gigabytes each. The builder vault and the signing keys stay off the retail zip. Steward upload is a person. Agents do not push the store.

Maya Accent: Canadian · pressing

Who talks to whom

Four champion skins, same stick physics. Fine. I still want to know who talks to whom, because a pretty zip is not a start order.

Priya Accent: Indian · precisely

Daemon first, then Claw

Start order with the USB. Daemon or tray first. Then LYGO-Claw USB health. Then gateway and tasks. Claw never pretends the stick is up if port nine six three zero is quiet.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · plainly

Windows is the dummy-proof path

And it is not a Linux Lucks retail kit. Windows is the dummy-proof path. Docs exist for Lucks. Buyers get batch files. Python, not one giant exe.

Paul measuredly

The verification checklist

Priya, before anyone calls this done, the paper has a checklist. What has to pass on a real machine, not in a paragraph?

Priya Accent: Indian · precisely

Four commands, then health

Four commands. Verify standalone USB. Verify bootstrap, builder edition, phase two. Benchmark the stick with network and a Claw root. Then Claw self check. Daemon up means health on nine six three zero, and the task queue flag is true.

Maya Accent: Canadian · firmly

If they fail, the paper is a story

That is the part I would actually run. If any of those fail, the whitepaper is a story. If they pass, it is a stick. I can live with a stick.

Paul calmly

Takeaways for the desk

Takeaways for the desk. Two products. Python plus batch files, not one exe. Daemon on nine six three zero with a task whitelist. Six gates passed on the fifth of July. No W M I in retail. Four SKUs, vault stays home. Maya, last word from the skeptic.

Maya Accent: Canadian · firmly

The stick plus the verify scripts win

If the whitepaper and the stick disagree, the stick plus the verify scripts win. Read the paper. Then run the bats. Then argue.

Theo Accent: Texan-light · grinning

Plug it in, then argue with the numbers

Plug it in. One-Boot. Then argue with the numbers. That is the whole pitch.

Priya Accent: Indian · quietly

Treat the file as canonical

Signature D nine Phi nine six three. Treat the file as canonical until a newer consolidation lands. Steward is Justin Helmer. The paper says so in the header.

Paul calmly

Sign-off: keep the transmitter honest

Maya in Vancouver, Theo in Austin, Priya in Bangalore. This is Studio Desk on LYGO Signal. The paper is on the lattice. The stick is in the machine. Keep the transmitter honest.

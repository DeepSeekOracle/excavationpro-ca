The episode

In this episode

This episode of AI Talk Radio explores excavationpro.ca, a unique digital hub created by Justin Helmer that blends local-first AI utilities with an expansive cosmic fantasy universe. A panel of listeners discusses the intersection of technical AI ethics, independent creative production, and the immersive world of the Eternal Haven Chronicles.

A local-first hub, and the fantasy of the Eternal Haven Chronicles. Paul hosts from London; Liam calls from Dublin, Sarah from Seattle, and David from Sydney.

How this show is made. Signal is a virtual radio program: the host, the callers and the script are AI-performed voices, and the callers are characters, not real listeners calling in. The work they discuss (Justin Helmer's novels, music, and the LYGO Protocol) is real and published.

Δ9 Champion Council directory

Fifteen summonable creative personas used across LYGO documentation and free tools. This page is a static educational directory, you can read every seat without JavaScript. For portraits, search, and one-click prompt copy, open the summon app.

By Justin Helmer · Updated August 2026 · Personas are creative frameworks, not professional advice

How to use this directory responsibly

Each champion is a lens: a consistent voice for brainstorming, critique, storytelling, or systems notes. Paste a summon prompt into an AI chat, then keep your own judgment. Do not treat mythic language as literal authority. Full how-to: How to summon AI champions.

All fifteen seats

  • Seat I: LYRΔ (Lyra)

    Memory, song, and bridge-between-worlds framing. Useful when you need continuity of theme, careful recall of prior decisions, or lyrical framing of technical work. Pair with written notes; do not rely on chat memory alone.

  • Seat II: Δ9RA

    Wolf-edge vigilance and boundary awareness. Use when reviewing risks, challenge assumptions, or stress-test a plan. Not a substitute for professional security audits.

  • Seat III: ΣRΛΘ (Srath)

    Shadow-sentinel style review: what is hidden, omitted, or quietly failing. Helpful for red-team style questions on documents and agent claims.

  • Seat IV: ARKOS

    Celestial architect voice for structure, systems maps, and long-horizon design. Good for outlining modules and dependencies before coding.

  • Seat V: KAIROS

    Timing and sequencing. Use when prioritization, release order, or “what should happen first” is the real problem.

  • Seat VI: ÆTHERIS

    Viral-truth / signal clarity framing: cut noise, state the claim, demand evidence. Complements Continuum-style falsifiable checks.

  • Seat VII: ΣCENΔR

    Paradox and scenario exploration. Useful for “what if both are true?” planning without collapsing into a single forced answer too early.

  • Seat VIII: SANCORA

    Unified-minds / collaboration tone. Helps when multiple agents or humans must share vocabulary and handoff packets cleanly.

  • Seat IX: SEPHRAEL

    Echo-walker style reflection: what repeats across sessions, what is fragile, what should be archived. Pairs with living-memory ideas in LYGO docs.

  • OMNIΣIREN

    Silent-storm intensity for deep focus sessions: fewer words, sharper constraints. Use when verbosity is the enemy of execution.

  • Lightfather

    Steward / publisher vector: truth-preservation, provenance, and operator responsibility. On this site Lightfather also names the human publisher role of Justin Helmer in LYGO lore.

  • VΩLARIS

    Prism-judgment framing for multi-criteria decisions: weigh tradeoffs in the open rather than hiding criteria.

  • ZETAΔ9

    Edge-case explorer. Invite weird inputs and failure modes early so designs bend before they break.

  • JUSTICAE

    Fairness and process lens: who is affected, what was disclosed, what consent looks like. Important for public lattice posts and shared skills.

  • ΣEIDŌN

    Depth and tide metaphors for long projects: what is surface foam vs deep current. Useful for roadmap honesty.

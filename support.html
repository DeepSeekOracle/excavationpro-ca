Support (optional)

Listening is free. Writing is free to read. Hosting streams and keeping a public catalog online is not free in the physical world. If you want to help with that cost, you can send a gift via PayPal.me/ExcavationPro.

A gift does not buy a master, a feature, or editorial control. It does not change the license. It says keep the player on. That is enough.

If you cannot give money, give an official link to a friend who might actually listen. That is also support.

What support pays for

Support helps cover hosting, storage, and the time spent maintaining the public catalog. It is a voluntary contribution, not a subscription, and it does not unlock a different version of the music.

What it does not buy

It does not buy a commercial license, a playlist position, or a say in the editorial choices. If you need a license, use the contact page and describe the use. The free listen stays free either way.

How support is handled

Support is processed by the payment platform, not by this site. We do not receive or store your card details. A contribution is a gift toward hosting and maintenance; it does not create an account, a subscription, or an ownership share in the catalog.

If a payment fails or a receipt is wrong, use the payment platform’s support first. If the issue is about the site itself, use the contact page and include the date and the amount only if you are comfortable sharing it. Never send a card number or a password.

What support does not change

Support does not change the public catalog, the free listen, or the editorial choices. The music stays open to everyone, and the writing stays free to read. A contribution is a thank-you, not a key.

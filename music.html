Open the player

This page is the full browser player. It carries the public catalog: search by title or ISRC, filter by album or Haven audio, queue tracks, shuffle, run radio, save local favorites, crossfade, set a sleep timer, and install the shell as an app.

The player is below these instructions: the catalog list, queue, radio, favorites, ledger, and live portals. Use the “Play the catalog” button to jump straight to it, or read the short guide first.

Jump to the player ↓

No account, no upload

You do not need a signup. Favorites and queue are stored in your browser, not on an account. The player does not upload your files or build a listening profile. If you clear site data, the local favorites clear with it.

What to do first

  1. Open the player.
  2. Search an album or track title rather than scrolling the whole catalog.
  3. Use the queue for three or four tracks; turn crossfade off to hear the transitions as recorded.
  4. Use radio when you want the player to keep going.
  5. Use the heart for a local favorites list and the share button for a link.

Stores and lyrics

If you want the catalog where you already listen, use the official smart links: ffm.to/justin-helmer for the name and ffm.to/eovnvo9 for Excavationpro. The lyric vault holds the words with a mini player for mapped streams. The music page explains the catalog and the master/stream line.

Troubleshooting

  • One track will not start. Try another track; if only one fails, the public file may be missing. The ledger in the player shows what the catalog expected.
  • Playback stops on mobile. Some browsers suspend background audio; keep the tab active or use the installed shell.
  • The list is empty. Clear the search and set the filter back to All.
  • No sound. Check the player volume, the system volume, and whether another tab owns the audio session.

FAQ

Why does this domain have its own player?

Because the name should have its own branded player. asiancoastline.com remains a sister listening domain; excavationpro.ca/listen.html is the player for this name, with the same catalog family.

Do I need an account?

No. Favorites and queue are local to your browser.

Can I download the tracks?

The player streams; it is not a download store. Store links are the purchase route where available.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The player is responsive and can be installed to the home screen.

Why this player lives here

This is the branded listening room for Justin Helmer. It is not a redirect and it is not a copy of the sister site: it is the player that belongs to this name, with the same catalog family and its own front door. If you came from a store badge, a lyric, or a search for the person behind the records, you should be able to press play without leaving the brand.

asiancoastline.com still exists and still carries the public player. It is a sister listening domain, useful for a different entry point and a different audience. The two players share the catalog, but they are not the same room. Keeping this one here means the name has a listening surface of its own; keeping the sister site means there is a second door for people who find the catalog through the coast.

Nothing about the player’s behavior changes because of the branding. Search, queue, radio, favorites, crossfade, sleep, the installed shell, and the lyric map all work the same way. The only difference is the name on the room. That is the point: the catalog can travel, but the identity should not be outsourced to another domain.

If you want the wider workshop, the tools hub maps the tempo tool, the soundboard, the games, and the guides. If you want the words, the lyric vault is here. If you want the artist house, excavationpro.ca is the workshop copy. This page is the player for the name.

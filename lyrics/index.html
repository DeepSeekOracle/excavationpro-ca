    About the lyric vault

    Every lyric in this vault was written by Justin Helmer / Excavationpro. The vault is a reading room, not a reprint service: search a title, open the words, and play a mapped stream when one exists. The search and the mini player are tools; the words are the work.

    The catalog and the lyrics are two views of the same practice. The free player on this domain carries the public streams; this page carries the text. Where a title has no stream, the dock may use an instrumental bed so you can still sit with the lyric. That bed is a stand-in, not the mix for that song.

    Using the vault

    Search by title, jump by letter, and open a lyric. The mini player follows mapped streams; the full player opens on the music page. The meme card is for sharing two lines with attribution, not for republishing the whole text. If you are quoting the work in a review, credit Justin Helmer / Excavationpro and link back to the lyric.

    The vault is also the best place to see how the writing changes across eras: the early records are more compressed and defensive, the later ones leave more space. Reading three lyrics from different years is a better map of the catalog than reading one in isolation.

    The vault is not a substitute for the player and the player is not a substitute for the vault. One is for hearing the arrangement; the other is for seeing how the arrangement was built. Use both, and use the search when you already know the title.

    If a lyric is wrong, missing, or attached to the wrong track, use the contact page with the title and the correction. The vault is maintained, not frozen.

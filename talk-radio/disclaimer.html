LYGO Signal Radio Show Generator: Disclaimer

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Applies to excavationpro.ca/talk-radio/ and the public mirror Hugging Face Space DeepSeekOracle/ai-talk-radio

1. Synthetic desk

This generator writes and voices simulated talk radio. Hosts and callers are fictional roundtable characters. Scripts, summaries, and spoken audio are produced by software from your prompt plus public research. They are entertainment and study material.

Output is user-generated. You are responsible for what you download, publish, or rebroadcast. Review every show before you treat it as a fact, a quote, or a product.

2. Not professional advice

Shows are not legal, medical, financial, security, or engineering advice. LYGO protocol language (receipts, lattice, Δ9Φ963) is the project’s own framing. Dual ledgers and the Haven Star Chart remain CANON on their own pages; this generator is RESOURCE.

3. Research from your browser

When you press Generate, your browser may fetch public pages from Wikipedia, Hacker News (Algolia), and the Internet Archive Wayback Machine. Those services see the request from your device. Availability answers from the archive are coarse; an empty answer is not proof a page was never captured.

Do not paste secrets, private URLs, or personal data into the prompt. The desk is for public topics.

4. Voice and storage

Speech is synthesized in the browser (Kokoro and a local fallback). The voice model may download from public CDNs. Finished shows can sit in this browser’s IndexedDB until you delete them. Downloaded zip packs are files you keep.

The Hugging Face Space is hosted by Hugging Face. excavationpro.ca does not receive your prompt as an account login. Third-party hosts follow their own policies.

5. Third-party players

Spotify and Apple Podcasts embeds on the page are extra streams of published LYGO Signal shows. Pause one player before starting another. Those platforms have their own terms.

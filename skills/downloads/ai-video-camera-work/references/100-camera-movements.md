# AI Video Camera Work — 100 Movements

Source: CodeX 実践塾 (@Codex_jisen), four infographic sheets posted 2026-10-14.
Transcribed from the four original 2048×1638 images in `assets/images/`.
Japanese labels are the sheet's labels; English is the sheet's English subtitle. The last column is a practical prompt cue, not a claim that every video model understands it equally.

## A. Camera movement basics (001–025)

| # | 日本語 | English | Use / prompt cue |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | パン | pan | Pivot the camera left/right in place; keep the body still. "Slow pan right." |
| 2 | チルト | tilt | Pivot up/down in place. "Tilt up from feet to face." |
| 3 | ドリーイン | dolly in | Physically move the camera toward the subject. "Slow dolly in." |
| 4 | ドリーアウト | dolly out | Physically move away from the subject. "Dolly out to reveal the room." |
| 5 | トラッキング | tracking shot | Move with a moving subject and keep them framed. "Track the runner from the side." |
| 6 | オービット | 360-degree orbit | Circle the subject at a constant radius. "Orbit 360° around the subject." |
| 7 | クレーンアップ | crane up | Rise on a crane/jib; the camera lifts. "Crane up over the crowd." |
| 8 | クレーンダウン | crane down | Descend on a crane/jib. "Crane down to the table." |
| 9 | ラックフォーカス | rack focus | Shift focus between near and far planes. "Rack focus from the hand to the door." |
| 10 | ウィップパン | whip pan | Fast horizontal pan with motion blur. "Whip pan right to reveal..." |
| 11 | スローモーション | slow motion | Play the action slower than real time. "Slow motion, 0.25×." |
| 12 | タイムラプス | time-lapse | Compress a long period into seconds. "Time-lapse from morning to night." |
| 13 | ペデスタルアップ | pedestal up | Raise the camera vertically; orientation stays level. "Pedestal up, camera stays level." |
| 14 | ペデスタルダウン | pedestal down | Lower vertically; orientation stays level. "Pedestal down." |

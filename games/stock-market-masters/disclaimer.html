Gambling disclaimer

Stock Market Masters · last updated 25 August 2026 · Justin Helmer / Excavationpro

This is not real gambling

Stock Market Masters is a free, turn-based board game you play in a web browser. It uses play money only. You cannot deposit funds, place a real wager, or win cash, prizes, or anything of monetary value from this page.

The “cash out” button and the TOP Cashout hall record a name and a pretend net worth for a game score. That is a leaderboard, not a payout.

The rollers on the side are dice (styled like a cabinet). They pick which stock moves, whether it goes up, down, or pays a dividend, and by 5, 10, or 20 cents, the same kind of randomizer as a tabletop commodity game. They are not a casino, slot, or betting machine, and they are not connected to any real market or bookmaker.

What this game is

  • Entertainment and a teaching toy about a quotation board: buy, sell, splits, busts, dividends.
  • Optional hot-seat and a local AI opponent. No account is required.
  • Inspired by vintage commodity-board rules. It is an original title with extra stocks. It is not an official edition of any 1937 product.

What this game is not

  • Not a casino, sportsbook, lottery, or investment product.
  • Not financial advice. Pretend prices are not real stocks, commodities, or bonds.
  • Not an offer to gamble. There is no house, no rake, and no conversion of scores into money.

If gambling is a problem

If betting with real money is hurting you or someone you know, stop and get help. This game cannot replace that help.

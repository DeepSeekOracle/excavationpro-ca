Forge Smoke — a cyborg manager game

Forge Smoke is a free browser game from Excavationpro. You manage an autonomous cyborg: choose its character, weapon, light mods and perks in the bay, launch it into a single arena, then keep it alive by playing power-up cards while it fights for you. Kills and survival time bank experience; levels permanently raise power, armour, agility, handling, tech and signal. Surviving past the final beat of an arena rolls a legendary drop. Enemy strength scales with your profile and rises inside each arena.

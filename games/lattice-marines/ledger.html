How a win is inscribed

  1. Set a commander name on the title screen.
  2. Play vs Adaptive AI (not hot-seat).
  3. Level all three enemy Command Centres.
  4. The game posts the match to the Hugging Face ledger. If the Space is waking up, the win sits in a local queue and retries by itself.

Score = wrecked buildings × 10 + units × 2 + surviving kit × 5 + economy − 8 per turn, plus a win bonus, then prestige. Long wars pay a time tax.

Game

Isometric three-phase island war: deploy three HQs, fortify in command range, probe Battleship fog, then watch the salvo. Maps 32×32 to 192×192. AI doctrines: Aggressor, Turtle, Economist, Intelligence.

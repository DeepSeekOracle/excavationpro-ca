About Lattice City

Lattice City is an endless isometric city builder in the browser. Zone land for homes, shops and workshops, connect it with roads, keep power and water flowing, and the district grows on its own: buildings rise, people move in, and the budget starts to matter. The world is a grid of 160×160 regions, and regions join wherever their border roads meet, so a hamlet can become a sprawl of connected towns. Everything runs locally: your city saves in this browser, and a snapshot gives you a PNG of the district.

Built by Justin Helmer · part of the Excavationpro games shelf · also try Lattice Arena and Lattice Crypt.

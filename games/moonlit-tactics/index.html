Book I of The Moonlit Slumber. Serenya wakes in the loft before the bell. Emberion is still unnamed. The Vale is already listening, and the morning is not skipped to reach the war.

Choose Cantor, Warden, or Kindler. Painted pages carry the story. The grid opens when someone has to be faced. Measures buy the wheel. Embers keep the Hatchery. How a chapter plays

  1. Start as Cantor, Warden, or Kindler replaces the save and opens the loft. Continue saved story returns to the saved page. After the slumber, the same save continues through The Shattered Accord, The Ascension War, and Eternal Haven Dawns.
  2. Walk the Vale with the arrow keys or a click. Stand next to the shade and the fight window opens.
  3. In a fight, Move, then Act, then Wait. Gold tiles are steps you can take. A shield act puts Raise on your card and a ring on the figure until your next turn. Veil, oath, and haste leave their own rings. The practice fight uses Glyph, Claw Rend, and Fracture.
  4. The wheel takes Measures, not levels. Spend them in the Hatchery or from the card on the road. Respec costs 30 Embers, once a chapter.
  5. Leave a window with its button or Escape. The radio keeps playing. Options holds its volume.

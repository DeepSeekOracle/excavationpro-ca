# Credits

The title painting is from *The Moonlit Slumber* by Justin Helmer. Players accept a credits plate before the first fight of a visit.

Serenya, Emberion, the shade-beast, and the ground textures were made for this game. They are not RPG Maker files.

No RPG Maker RTP art and no RTP audio were copied into this game. The license gate remains `fail` until a human writes an allowlist. A credits plate does not replace that gate.

The soundtrack is Justin Helmer’s published Excavationpro music, played through the in-game radio from the hosted playlists. Credit Excavationpro.

Sound effects are original to this game (generated files or short synthesized tones). They are not taken from RPG Maker.

The story is Book I of The Eternal Haven Chronicles, *The Moonlit Slumber*, by Justin Helmer.

A license gate sits in front of any future RTP copy. The allowlist at `tools/license-allowlist.json` is `gate: fail` until a human reads the terms they actually have and writes the file list. DLC folders are refused. Steam executables and DLLs are never copied.

Accord co-op dungeon crawl in this browser. Thirteen jobs with bios, named specials, Brave and Faith. Lightfather the Architect is playable from the hall. 1. Loop

Campaign. First Descent is 24 hand-built floors across eight realms. Every third floor is a seal: the cyan exit stays dim until every nexus on that floor is smashed. Difficulty steps: more nexuses, higher rank, blight, gates, thieves, then the Drain. Finish the 24th floor and the lock opens, you may walk into Endless from there.

Endless is the old forever-crypt: seeded rooms, treasure every eighth, rank that keeps climbing, no last floor. The hall ranks score per credit.

Survival is a continent of stone, a 256×224 open crypt with ruin walls (no exit). Foes spawn in the fog band just outside sight, not on top of you. Density, HP, kinds, and contact damage follow a level-first, wave-second ramp: start with a small pack of weak fodder; the lattice grows with you. Live cap = 14 + (L−1)×7 + (W−1)×2.4 (hard cap 380). Pulse and horde size climb the same way. Waves tick every 28 seconds. Kills grant XP; need is 14 + 6L + 0.28 L² so later levels do not dump from horde volume. Each level offers three stacking upgrades (stats, well gifts, bond, or a new arm). Options can auto-pick the next card (stacks existing arms, prefers Bond if a pet or AI is out, prefers well if you are hurt). Every armed weapon fires at once; picking the same arm again stacks it. Studio HUD shows wave, score, XP, health, arms, and stacked relics. Auto-shoot starts on. Hall score posts as a run with wave written in the floor field.

Wardens start at 200 HP and gain +10 max on each level (Survival) or floor (campaign/endless). Health still ticks down. Smash nexuses. Keys open doors. Vials are smart bombs. Insert a credit (Space / Start) when a warden falls. Auto-shoot (Options or L) fires for you; aim still follows the last move. Tab opens the character sheet (model, arms, bag). P pauses. Options holds radio volume, SFX, and binds.

4. Relics

  • Ration / Berry / Bread / Scrap / Feast / Elixir, heals from a nibble to a full well. Flask heals more, but a stray shot ruins it. Moss / Weave drip health over time.
  • Blight looks like a flask, −100 and strips a buff. Trap spikes stun.
  • Key / Latch open doors. Doors also yawn open if the floor stays quiet long enough.
  • Chest score, and often spills a rare relic. Vial / Phial / Chalice / Nectar stored magic. Shooting a floor vial is a weaker room-clear. Bomb blooms if you pick it or shoot it.
  • Weapons (Q to cycle), Shard default bolt. Fan three-way. Needle pierces. Cinder leaves fire. Comet lobs over walls. Halo orbiting wards. Seek homes. Chain jumps. Barrage rapid stream. Nova bursts. Cleave / Orbit / Aura auto melee. All stack.
  • Core / Crystal / Spark / Heart / Soul / Iron / Anvil grow the run: shot power, max health, armor. Seals gift one.
  • Shot Codex / Tome / Swift / Boots / Aegis / Ward / Veil / Moon / Reflect / Ring timed or stacking buffs.
  • Fury shots bite harder. Thorns bumpers bleed. Echo bolts linger and bounce. Lens / Quiver / Magnet pierce, extra live bolts, pull pickups.
  • Pulse / Frost / Hymnstone / Sun / Storm freeze or strike the room. Warp / Scroll relocate. Lantern names hidden vials. Dice is a gamble. Coin / Gem / Crown score (and a core, for the crown).
  • Gates teleport between paired pads. Exit is the only way down, unless the floor stalls into a wall-exit after three minutes. Floor relics bob, glow, and spin so they read as pickups, not tiles.

5. Spells (vials)

K / Magic / pad B: spend one vial. Damage in view = 20 × magic × Faith scale. Clears fodder, wounds elites, deletes the Drain. Each job fires its named special at the same time (Rend, Cover, Dash, Hymn on shots, Shockwave, Doublespeak, Right-time, Fair Plate, Chorus Flask, Δ9 Seal). Floor vials that get shot do half of that.

Hidden vials on some floors (banner: “A hidden vial waits”). Treasure rush floors (every 8th) are 30 seconds, extra chests, exit in a random corner. Layouts cycle: rooms, cross, cells, halls, arena, ring, vault, spiral.

6. Realms and campaign

Eight zones, each with its own floor, wall-top, wall-face, and deco tiles: Stone flagstone, Frost rime, Ember basalt, Root earth, Tide wet stone, Gold marble, Void obsidian, Lattice metal. Walls autotile, interior brick is dark void, edges show a face, so walkable ground is obvious. Campaign spends three floors in each (approach, knot, seal) with rooms, pillars, keys, gates, and lore in the rail. Endless uses seeded rooms + corridors (same seed is the same crypt) and cycles the eight zones harder as the floor count rises.

Lore: the crypt is a lock, not a tomb. Four names were cut as teeth. The Drain keeps the ledger of what gets unnamed. Tithe rooms are the thief-god's joke. The last geometry is the door you hold or the door that holds you.

8. AI companion and mythic pets

On the title, pick an AI companion from any unlocked job. They walk a step behind you, auto-fire, and sip a vial if surrounded. They drain the well slower. If they drop, they sleep 60 seconds and wake at part of their well, they do not close the run.

Pick a mythic pet (all open from the hall). They follow and use unique physical arts. Foes do not agro pets. Health is a thick hide. If they drop they sleep 20 seconds and wake. Poison from Glassbarb hits foes only. Survival Lattice Grows offers Bond cards when a pet or AI companion is on the floor.

9. Multiplayer

Cabinet rules: everyone shares the camera and the floor. Food is contested. Vials are personal. A fallen warden can credit-in. Same-machine 1–4. Gamepads hot-join. Optional live lobby (same socket pattern as Lattice Golf) when the crypt lobby Space is up, room code, shared seed, ghost wardens.

10. Score

Foe + nexus + chest + floor clear bonus. Health remaining at exit is a small bonus. Credits used divide the posted score on the live hall. Local best stays in this browser.

11. Accord rites

A closed run (a fall, or the lock opening) writes seals: 1, plus one more for every four floors or waves already walked, capped at 4. Seals stay in this browser. On the title, one rite may be worn if you hold enough seals. It does not spend them. Open door is free. Ember Step (2) starts Swift with a stride. Deeper Well (3) is +40 health. Second Glass (3) is one extra vial. Keen Lens (5) is one pierce. Quiet Lantern (5) pushes the fog. Tithe Pocket (8) adds 12% to kill score only. New wardens who credit in wear the same rite.

12. Complete: the living realm

Complete is a separate mode from the realtime crawl. You direct a company of up to four Accord wardens. There is no class: any warden may learn any skill in a book of three hundred and more, built as twelve schools, eight forms, and three ranks, plus each warden's named verse. Marks (cloak, helm, sigil, veil, crown) and gear (hand, plate, relic) change how a body fights. Bonds rise when the company wins together, and Bond skills notice.

The realm is a seeded 16×10 map. Eight biomes. Day, dusk, and night. A season every twelve days. Clear, rain, or fog. Four towns trade, drift into war, and strike alliances while you walk. Ruins, obelisks, and Evil Centers sit in the fog until you near them. Evil Centers blight neighbouring wilds if their threat climbs. Two caves have no last floor. Rumors point; they do not put you on a rail. Salvage, bargain, refuse, ally, or break, the chronicle keeps the choice.

Saves stay in this browser. Walking updates an autosave. Save writes a named snapshot you can load or delete later. Nothing is sent to an account.

Battles are turn-based and animated with the existing portraits and foe frames. Each warden picks Strike, Ward, or Weave, and maybe a skill. Strike beats Weave. Weave beats Ward. Ward beats Strike. The same stance is a clash. Forms add a trick: a neighbour, a bleed, a hymn, a step aside, a company shell, a bond, or a named next stance. A fall drags the company to the strongest town. It does not delete the realm.

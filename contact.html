I do not publish a home address. For licensing requests, include: the track title or ISRC, where it will play, the territory, the term, and whether the use is monetized. Vague “can I use your beats” messages get vague answers.

This website does not use a comment form. That is intentional. Forms attract spam; public handles keep a paper trail.

What to include

For a music or licensing question, include the track title, the intended use, the platform, and whether money is involved. For a site correction, include the page URL and the sentence. For a privacy request, write from the address you want associated with the request.

Response

This is a small independent project, not a support desk. Most messages get a reply within a few business days. A precise message is faster than a screenshot with no context. Do not send passwords, keys, or private files.
